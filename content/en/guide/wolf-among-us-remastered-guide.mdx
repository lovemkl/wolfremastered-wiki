## What The Wolf Among Us Remastered Is

The original The Wolf Among Us (2013-2014) told the story of Bigby Wolf — the Big Bad Wolf of legend, now sheriff of Fabletown, the secret Manhattan community where fairy tale refugees live hidden among ordinary New Yorkers. When a murder lands on his desk, the investigation drags Bigby through the community's glamour-dealing underworld toward a confrontation that would define the series. It was Telltale at the height of its powers: a five-episode season of hard choices, harder violence and a noir atmosphere so complete that its 1980s New York felt like a character.

The Wolf Among Us Remastered brings that season back as the definitive edition. "Fairy tales are real. Murder is just the beginning" remains the pitch, and the remaster's job is to make good on it in 2026: rebuilt visuals, modern platform releases, and a new Noir Mode that plays the entire season in striking black and white. For players who never experienced the original, it is the best possible doorway into a classic; for players who did, it is the reason to walk Fabletown's streets again.

## Release Date and Platforms

The Wolf Among Us Remastered launches **October 29, 2026** on **PC and consoles** — Steam on PC, plus the PlayStation and Xbox console families, with the announcement trailer pegging the unlock at the evening of October 28 Pacific time on PlayStation. The remaster reintroduces the world to a new audience at exactly the moment the franchise's future is being built. Expect the buying guide logic to be simple: if you have never played the original, this is the version; if you have, the question is how much you want to revisit Fabletown looking its best.

## What the Remaster Upgrades

The remaster's upgrades cluster in four areas. **Visuals first**: rebuilt character models, lighting and environments bring the original's distinctive comic-noir art direction to modern standards — the cel-shaded grit that made Fabletown iconic, now rendered without 2013's compromises, on every platform the remaster ships on. **Performance and presentation**: modern console and PC targets, faster loading, and a technically smooth experience the episodic original never had at launch. **The complete season in one package**: all five episodes of the original season — Faith, Smoke and Mirrors, A Crooked Mile, In Sheep's Clothing and Cry Wolf — integrated into a single continuous experience without the episodic seams of the 2013-2014 rollout. **And Noir Mode**, the remaster's headline addition: a black-and-white presentation mode that strips the color from the season and doubles down on its detective-film DNA.

Telltale has framed the remaster as "the definitive way to step back into Fabletown," and the framing holds: this is preservation plus polish, not reinvention. The choices, the story and the structure are the ones players remember — on hardware that does them justice.

## Noir Mode: The Headline Feature

Noir Mode deserves its own paragraph because it is the remaster's one genuinely new idea. Playing The Wolf Among Us Remastered in Noir Mode filters the entire season to black and white with contrast pushed to detective-film extremes — rain-slick streets, sodium-lamp glow rendered in grayscale, blood that lands as pure black shadow. It is a presentation option, not a separate campaign, but it is one with a point of view: the game was always a noir; the mode simply stops pretending otherwise. First-playthrough players can choose either presentation — the full-color original look or Noir Mode — and completionists will want a second run in the other. ## FAQ

**When does The Wolf Among Us Remastered release?** October 29, 2026, on PC (Steam) and consoles, with PlayStation unlocking the evening of October 28 Pacific time per the announcement.

**Do I need to play anything before it?** No — The Wolf Among Us Remastered is the complete original season, self-contained. Knowledge of the Fables comics enriches references but the game explains its world on its own terms.

**Is this the same as The Wolf Among Us 2?** No — the remaster is the 2013 season rebuilt; The Wolf Among Us 2 is the separately developed sequel. The remaster is the best way to be caught up for whatever the sequel brings.

**What does the remaster include?** All five original episodes — the complete season — with rebuilt visuals, modern performance, and the new black-and-white Noir Mode.

**Is it worth replaying if I finished the original?** For Noir Mode alone, arguably yes; for the visual rebuild and a return to one of the genre's best stories, definitely. Bring your memory of your choices — the story is built to be seen twice.
